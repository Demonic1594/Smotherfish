Help test Smotherfish

Lend your GitHub Actions minutes; nothing runs on your computer
1
Fork the repository

Keep the smotherfish branch.

2
Enable workflows

In your fork's Actions tab (GitHub turns them off on new forks).

3
Add the owner as collaborator

Then tests start on your fork automatically within about 15 minutes. Full guide

Test data is collected every 15 minutes by the repository's "Status data" workflow; results and verdicts are added after each analysis. Smotherfish is free software under the GPL v3, built on Stockfish.
